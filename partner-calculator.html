How the money flows

CET Global is the pre-approved consultant named in the client's application, so the client's payments and the grant both come to CET Global. The grant is paid by the Programme Partner (SBF or SNEF) only after it has checked the deliverables of each milestone; allow 6 to 8 weeks for each grant payment.

The partner's share

Always a percentage of the project sum before GST. 9% GST is billed to the client in full with the client's first invoice and is not shared. If the partner is GST-registered, it adds GST to its own invoices to CET Global on top of its share.

How each payout is made

CET Global tells the partner when money comes in and shares the remittance advice. The partner invoices CET Global for that payout; CET Global pays within the agreed number of days of receiving cleared funds. No payout is due on money that has not reached CET Global.

Before the final payout

The final grant is released only when all SWDA-mandated documents are in: Pre-JRET, kick-off deck, completion report, Post-JRET, completion survey and the success story with the client's testimonial and photographs. A late or missing document delays the grant, and so the final payout.

If the grant is reduced or clawed back

Where this is caused by one party, that party bears it. Otherwise each party bears it in proportion to its share, and any amount already paid out on money that is later clawed back is refunded to CET Global within 30 days.

If the scope changes

If the Programme Partner approves a different project sum after Milestone 1, both shares are re-worked on the revised sum and the difference is settled at the Final Milestone.
