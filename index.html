2Good to know

Before you apply

Do not pay CET Global anything, and do not sign any contract or purchase order, until the grant application has been submitted on the Business Grants Portal. Payments or contracts made before submission make the project ineligible. If you intend to use Enhanced SFEC, indicate it on the application form.

How the grant is paid

The grant never passes through your hands. The Programme Partner (SBF or SNEF) pays it directly to CET Global, so you pay only your share, plus GST, and there is no grant claim for you to file. Each grant payment is released only after the Partner has checked and approved the deliverables of that milestone.

Payment schedules CET Global may offer

The standard schedule, and the minimum in CET Global's quotation and invoicing, is 2 payments of 30% / 70%, matching SWDA's milestones. Case by case, to suit a project's scope, effort and resourcing, CET Global may agree:

  • 2 payments · 50% as first payment, 50% as final payment
  • 3 payments · 30% / 40% / 30%, with an interim stage agreed in the quotation

These are private arrangements between you and CET Global. The Programme Partner still pays the grant only at the two SWDA milestones, 30% then 70%.

SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit (SFEC) in brief

Enhanced SFEC, from 1 Dec 2026: eligible companies receive a fresh S$10,000 credit in an online wallet (complete a short workforce questionnaire to unlock it). It offsets up to 90% of your share up front instead of a refund: you pay your share after the offset, and the Programme Partner pays the grant plus your SFEC to CET Global once it approves the completion report. Credits are applied all-or-nothing up to your balance and expire on 30 Nov 2029.

Current SFEC, until 30 Nov 2026: a refund, one claim per project. 90% of your whole share if the project is completed and approved on the portal at least a week before 30 Nov 2026; otherwise 90% of your first payment only.

GST

The grant supports the project cost only; GST is not supported. 9% GST on the full project cost is payable by you, and CET Global bills it with your first payment. No GST is charged on the grant, and SFEC does not offset GST.

If the scope changes

If the approved project cost goes up or down after Milestone 1, the grant, your share and any SFEC amount are re-worked pro-rata on the revised cost and the difference is settled at the Final Milestone. Excess SFEC goes back to your wallet.

Who does what

CET Global prepares the detailed project plan and the completion report. At each milestone it invoices you for your share and the Programme Partner for the grant portion; the Partner checks the deliverables before releasing the grant. Timing of the milestones follows the project plan in your proposal.
